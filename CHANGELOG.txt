CHANGE LOG (newest first) - compared with erp_t.zip as uploaded

12. Vacation accrual and carry-over (VAC-01) - built on the existing logic
   - Accrued: now adds up every period (vacation % x gross + accrual
     adjustments). Before, it stayed at period 1's amount all year.
   - Carry-over: last year's CLOSING balance is the "balance forward" all year.
     Before, period 1 carried last year's accrued total (vacation already taken
     or paid out was carried again) and from period 2 the carry-over vanished.
   - Used: vacation payouts + vacation pay taken (Payout treatment: the
     accrual) + "vacation used" adjustments. Balance adjustments stay in the
     balance forward.
   - Balance = forward + accrued - used on every stub (the formula the code
     already used for period 1). Extra runs, re-runs, out-of-order runs and
     pay-schedule changes give the same balances.
   - Unchanged: the per-period calculation (calcVacBalance), sick balances.
   - Stubs processed before this fix carry their stored closing balance into
     next year; if one is wrong, enter a vacation balance adjustment once on
     the first pay run of the year.
   - NOT changed, needs your decision (docs/CHANGES.md VAC-02): with the
     "Payout" treatment the 4 % is shown as paid but is not added to gross pay.
   - Tests: T-VAC-01..07 (fail on the previous code), 474 server tests pass.

11. Full bug review (three independent reviews, every finding checked and tested)
   - Year-to-date totals (and the CPP / EI annual maximums) are now the year's
     earlier pay stubs added up, in pay-date order. Before, they continued from
     "period N-1" only, which: double-counted a re-run extra run; restarted the
     year after an extra run in period 1, an unpaid period or a pay-schedule
     change; and could lose an extra run paid between two regular runs.
     A full simulated year of real pay runs now ends exactly on the 2026
     maximums (CPP 4,646.45 incl. CPP2, EI 1,123.07).
   - Pay stub e-mails: one bad stub or a database error no longer stops the
     batch or fails the whole Send Paystub request; a stub already being sent
     shows "already being sent"; accented names kept in PDF file names.
   - Stat holidays: cannot be filled in twice (database guard); duplicate names
     answer "already exists" instead of an error.
   - Time clock: early-morning punches on a stat holiday are no longer moved to
     the previous day (a mistake in an earlier change of mine).
   - Employee pickers: a deleted employee in the list no longer crashes the
     screen; the accounting ledger filter can untick rows again.
   - Holiday page shows any selected year; credential e-mails warn about names
     not found; undated announcements listed last.
   - Vacation accrual: fixed in 12.

10. CPP / EI / income tax now follow the CRA formulas (T4127)
   - CPP by year and stopped at the annual maximum (was never capped); CPP2
     added (4% between $74,600 and $85,000 in 2026). 2027: CPP rate 5.75%.
   - EI by year and stopped at the annual maximum (was capped per pay at the
     2023 maximum). 2027: 1.64% up to $70,800.
   - TD1: a blank or 0 tax credit (the screen's default) now means the basic
     personal amount ($16,452 federal / $13,216 BC in 2026). Before, it meant
     none, about $83 too much tax per $2,000 bi-weekly pay. New "Claim code 0"
     checkbox for employees who claim nothing (e.g. a second job).
   - CPP, EI and the Canada employment amount are credited, and enhanced CPP
     is deducted from taxable income, as CRA requires.
   - BC tax reduction applied (low earners pay less BC tax).
   - Staying current: run  cd server && npm run check:rates  - it lists what is
     loaded / missing for this year and next, and sample deductions to compare
     with the CRA online calculator (PDOC). The server logs a warning every day
     from Nov 1 while next year is incomplete, and npm test fails in December
     until next year's tables are in. Calendar: docs/TECHNICAL.md 1.14.
   - Due now: 2027 federal tables and 2027 CPP YMPE/YAMPE (published November).

9. Alerts and Violations duplicates + Support panel for every company
   - Duplicates: an alert was saved once per pay period, but the list ignores
     the period, so the same problem showed once per pay run. Now one alert per
     employee and problem, updated on every inputs review and removed when
     fixed. Old copies are removed at the next inputs review (or right away
     with: node scripts/resolve-duplicates.js --apply).
   - Minimum wage check uses BC's current rate: 8.25 since June 1, 2026 (was
     7.85). A rate of exactly the minimum now clears the alert.
   - Support panel: general notices now show for every company (Cornerstone
     saw none: the messages only existed for BusinessN). Each notice shows its
     own text. Notices: BC tax change July 2026, minimum wage June 2026, moving
     a statutory holiday. Edit server/constants/supportNotices.constants.js.

8. Tax tables by pay date + BC statutory holidays by year
   - BC income tax: was 5.06% for every pay date. Now 5.06% until June 30, 2026,
     6.14% from July 1, 2026 (CRA July 2026 tables: BC Budget 2026 made 2026
     5.60%), 5.60% from 2027 with 2026 thresholds (frozen 2027-2030).
   - Federal: 2026 brackets confirmed (14/20.5/26/29/33%; 58,523 / 117,045 /
     181,440 / 258,482). 2025 added (15% Jan-Jun, 14% Jul-Dec). 2027 not
     published by CRA yet (November): the 2026 table is used and the server
     logs "TAX-TABLES: no federal table for 2027" until it is added.
   - Tables are picked by the pay date (CRA rule), so past, current and future
     pay dates each use their own year: server/constants/taxTables.constants.js.
     Update it each January and July (steps in docs/TECHNICAL.md 1.14).
   - Stat holidays: BC holidays calculated for any year and filled in
     automatically for an empty year (holiday page or daily job); year picker
     on the holiday page; editing a date into another year moves the holiday;
     evening punches on a stat now count as stat work (Vancouver day).

7. E-mail wording: pay date vs actual deposit
   - New note in every pay stub e-mail: "Please note: the pay date shown on your
     pay stub is the scheduled pay date for this pay period. The date the funds
     arrive in your bank account may be different, depending on when the payment
     is processed and on your bank's processing times."
   - "Your pay ... was issued on <date>" is now "Your pay stub for pay period ...
     is attached" with a "Pay date" line; subject "(paid <date>)" is now
     "(pay date <date>)". The e-mail no longer claims the money was paid that day.
